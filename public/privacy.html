Study App privacy

Study App is a personal study tool made and used by one student. It is not offered to the public.

What it stores

  • Flashcards, practice questions, homework and study plans are kept in your own browser on your own device.
  • The server keeps homework added from the phone page until the laptop collects it, and the Google sign-in token needed to sync your calendar. These are stored on the app's own server and nowhere else.

Google Calendar

  • The app reads the list of your calendars, and events from the work and timetable calendars you choose, to work out when you can study.
  • It only writes to the two calendars it creates itself (Study App: Due dates and Study App: Study blocks). It cannot change or delete anything else.
  • Calendar data is never sold, shared with anyone, or used for anything except planning your study.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Press Disconnect Google in the app (Planner, Phone and Google) to delete the stored sign-in, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Clearing the browser's site data removes everything stored on your device.
